## 2026-11-02 — The EE FPU without changing the rounding mode

- Add, subtract and multiply took the EE's rounding toward zero by loading MXCSR twice per operation. Now they round to nearest in the thread's own mode, take the exact rounding error (Fast2Sum for sums, a fused multiply-add for products, where the processor has FMA) and step one unit toward zero where rounding went outward; results near the smallest normal number (a product's within 24 binades of it) and overflows keep the old path. A unit test against the MXCSR reference (special values and 4 million random pairs, bit-exact); the gate's scripted path's guest state identical on all 5,137 fields. The FPU's share of the game thread's working samples 7.2% to 3.6% (the CPU fight with HD and 60 fps at 16x).
