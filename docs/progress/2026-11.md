## 2026-11-02 — The flicker the user saw with 60 fps (open)

- The user saw slight flicker in that run's fight with 60 fps (the fighters' bodies, the lower half of the screen; in a slow animation, seams or patches of the ground jumping). Ruled out by tests: the second renderer's state (check mode pixel-exact with HD), the game's motion blur (no draw reads the picture shown before), bones (all pass the blend's rigid test; values left at the newer frame's draw nothing when collapsed with `BT2_VIF1_REPLACE`). Two blend rules (values that moved a little in an object's setup; camera-facing particles, found by bisecting a list's changes) were tried and taken out: the user saw no difference.
- New: `BT2_IN_BETWEEN_GIF_DUMP` writes the in-between renderer's draws in `BT2_GIF_DUMP`'s form; `gif_dump_draws.py` carries packets that go on in the next transfer. At draw level (flips 3,004 to 3,006) every moving draw is blended in the in-between picture; the unpaired draws are the clipper's fans at the screen's edges. Next for the flicker: a recording of the moment from the user.
- Checked: the VIF1 blend tests, the Release build; the gate.
